YT Save

Paste link → Choose quality → Save to Gallery

Works with youtube.com, youtu.be and Shorts
On iPhone: Download → Share → Save Video → Opens in CapCut

How to save (iPhone)

1

Paste link from YouTube → Tap Get Video

2

Pick quality (1080p recommended for CapCut)

3

Video opens → Tap Share icon ↗ → Save Video

4

Open CapCut → New Project → Video is in your Gallery!

How to save (Laptop)

1

Same steps — file saves directly to your Downloads folder as MP4

2

Drag into CapCut Desktop or Premiere directly

✂️

CapCut tip: 1080p MP4 is best for editing. If original is 4K, picking 1080p gives smaller file & faster import.
